About

Rooted in Irish Tradition.
Performing Across New York.

I have been playing the Great Highland Bagpipes since 2008, returning to active performance in 2018. Since then I have become a regular presence across the New York tri-state area's Irish and Irish-American community — performing at weddings, memorials, cultural ceremonies, and landmark public occasions.

My work is closely tied to New York's long-standing Irish traditions. Highlights include performing at the NASDAQ closing bell in recognition of the NYC St. Patrick's Day Parade, and at the Irish Embassy in New York in honor of the 2026 Grand Marshal Robert James McCann.

I perform with the County Cork Pipe and Drums — a band established in 1936 with deep ties to both County Cork and the New York Irish community. In 2026, I will return to Ireland to compete in the Fleadh Cheoil in Belfast.

In the News

On Stage, On the Street,
In the Record

Memorial · Denis Cashman
Denis Cashman Gravestone Unveiling
A memorial was unveiled in Boston honoring Denis Cashman, an Abbeyside man who sacrificed his life for Ireland. The project, led by descendants and supporters including Susan Sullivan and the Fenian Memorial Committee of America commemorates his legacy and connects his Irish roots with his final resting place in the U.S.
Ceremonial · Liam Lynch
Liam Lynch Centenary
In April 2023, Fermoy marked the centenary of General Liam Lynch's death with a commemorative parade featuring 12 pipe bands from Ireland and abroad. The County Cork Pipe Band led the tribute, highlighting the region's strong musical tradition while honoring Lynch's legacy in the Irish Civil War.
Parade · St. Patrick's Day
Sunnyside St. Pat's for All
A fixture in the parades that define New York's Irish calendar — from the NYC St. Patrick's Day Parade to events across Long Island and the Hudson Valley.
